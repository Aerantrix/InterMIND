<div class="gf-section gf-section-elevated">
  <div class="gf-container-narrow">
    <h2 class="gf-h2 gf-h2-center">Common questions about our corporate counsel services</h2>
    <p class="gf-sub gf-sub-center">Specific to UAE corporate work. General Golden Fish questions live on the <a href="/">homepage FAQ</a>.</p>
    <div class="gf-faq">

:::details Who leads the work on our matter?
Every engagement is led by a senior corporate counsel with deep UAE practice. Specialist counsel for M&A, structuring or shareholder matters is brought in in-house where the matter requires it. The lead counsel and team are listed in your engagement letter.
:::

:::details Do you handle international or only UAE matters?
We are UAE-based and lead UAE-side matters end-to-end. For cross-border deals (EU, UK, US, BVI, KSA) we co-counsel with selected local firms; you sign one engagement letter with Golden Fish and we coordinate the rest.
:::

:::details What's the engagement process?
1) Confidential consultation; 2) engagement letter and fixed-fee or hourly quote within 48h; 3) analysis and written advisory memo; 4) drafting and negotiation; 5) closing and handover with a 30-day post-engagement Q&A window included.
:::

:::details How does pricing work?
Three models: fixed-fee for predictable scope (drafting SHA, structuring memo, single-deal M&A), hourly for evolving matters (complex negotiations, multi-party restructuring), and retainer for ongoing counsel. The fee model is disclosed in the engagement letter before any work begins. [Full prepayment model →](/uae-business/benefits/prepayment-model)
:::

:::details Do you offer ongoing counsel (retainer)?
Yes. Retainer engagements are tiered (starter / standard / premium) with a defined monthly hour bank, priority response, and rollover rules. Best for groups that need a "GC on call" without an in-house hire.
:::

:::details How is confidentiality handled?
The initial consultation is confidential. An NDA is available before the call on request. Once engaged, the scope, communications and deliverables are governed by the engagement letter and our internal confidentiality rules.
:::

:::details Can you take over a matter another firm has started?
Yes — we take over ongoing structuring, M&A and shareholder matters where another adviser has begun but the client wants to change counsel. We do a confidential file review first and provide a written second-opinion memo before formally taking over.
:::
