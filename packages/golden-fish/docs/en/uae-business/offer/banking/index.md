<div class="gf-section gf-section-elevated">
  <div class="gf-container-narrow">
    <h2 class="gf-h2 gf-h2-center">Banking questions, answered</h2>
    <p class="gf-sub gf-sub-center">Specific to UAE corporate banking. General Golden Fish questions live on the <a href="/">homepage FAQ</a>.</p>
    <div class="gf-faq">

:::details What banks do you open accounts with?
We work with 50+ UAE banks and have direct relationships with relationship managers at Emirates NBD, FAB, ADCB, Emirates Islamic, Mashreq, RAKBANK and others. We shortlist 3–5 banks per case based on fit and approval probability — we don't submit to one and hope.
:::

:::details How long does the process take?
Free zone setup with clean SME: 4 weeks. High-risk cases (crypto, fintech): 6–8 weeks. Multi-jurisdiction or restructuring: 5–6 weeks. Timelines depend on case complexity and how quickly documents come back from your side.
:::

:::details What if my case is rejected by the bank?
Our quote covers re-submissions if the first attempt fails. We typically open with 3–5 banks in parallel to maximise approval odds, and our overall approval rate is 96%. If all banks reject (rare), we re-prepare the file and resubmit at no extra cost — provided the rejection isn't due to undisclosed information from your side.
:::

:::details Do I need to fly to Dubai for the process?
Most steps are remote — application, document prep, compliance pre-screening. You typically need to be in Dubai for the bank interview (3–5 days) and to sign for the account. Many banks now accept video interviews.
:::

:::details Can you open an account for crypto / high-risk business?
Yes. High-risk cases are about half of what we handle — crypto consulting, fintech, gaming, high-margin advisory. We restructure the application, prepare an enhanced due-diligence memo, and select banks with risk appetite for the activity. Approval rate for high-risk: 90%.
:::

:::details What documents do I need to provide?
Standard set: passport copies and CVs of UBOs and signatories, corporate documents (trade licence, MoA, share register), 6 months of personal and business bank statements, proof of address, and a clear description of business activity and source of funds. We send a complete checklist after the consultation.
:::

:::details Can you set up multi-currency or multi-bank accounts?
Yes. USD, EUR, AED and GBP are usually opened together at the main bank. Multi-bank setups (3+ banks across entities, for asset segregation or risk diversification) are coordinated as one structuring engagement.
:::

:::details What if I don't have a UAE company yet?
We can run banking in parallel with company setup, or as a standalone engagement if you already have a licence. If you're starting from zero, we recommend our <a href="/uae-business/offer/company-registration/">UAE company registration</a> service — banking is included in those packages.
:::
