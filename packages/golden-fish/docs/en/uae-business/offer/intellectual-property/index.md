:::details Can I register a trademark internationally from UAE?
Yes. UAE is a signatory to the Madrid Protocol, which lets you file in 100+ countries through a single WIPO application based on your UAE registration. We handle the UAE base filing plus the Madrid designation in one engagement — saving months of separate national filings.
:::

:::details How long does trademark registration take in UAE?
9–12 months on average from filing to certificate. The timeline is mostly driven by MoEC examination, the publication period, and any oppositions. We give you an honest timeline upfront — anyone promising "TM registered in a month" is misleading you.
:::

:::details What if someone has already registered my trademark in UAE?
Cancellation actions are available where the prior registration was filed in bad faith or where you have demonstrable prior use. We've reclaimed hijacked trademarks for several clients without them having to pay off the squatter — but the strategy depends on evidence and timing, so the earlier you act, the better.
:::

:::details Do I need to register software copyright in UAE if it's auto-protected?
Copyright exists automatically when the work is created — but registration with UAE MoEC creates an evidentiary presumption that strengthens enforcement. For SaaS and software companies entering the UAE market, registration is a small fixed fee that pays for itself the first time you need to enforce.
:::

:::details How do you handle GCC trademark registration?
We file directly in each GCC member state (Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar) plus the GCC TM Office where applicable. For multi-country GCC coverage we usually recommend Madrid Protocol if you also need EU/US — it's often cheaper than separate national filings.
:::

:::details Can you draft IP licensing agreements between my group companies?
Yes. Intra-group IP licensing is one of our core engagements — typically a UAE IP-holding entity licensing brand and software to operating entities in EU/US/elsewhere. We coordinate with your tax adviser so the royalty flow is transfer-pricing-aware from the start.
:::

:::details What's the difference between TM in mainland vs free zone?
A UAE trademark is registered nationally with MoEC — it protects you across both mainland and free zones. Your trade-licence location doesn't change the trademark right. What matters for enforcement is the registration itself, not which entity owns it.
:::

:::details Can you help with Amazon / Noon takedowns?
Yes. Once you have a registered trademark, we enrol your brand in Amazon Brand Registry and Noon's IP-protection programme, then run takedowns against infringing or counterfeit listings. For repeat-offender suppliers, we follow up with cease &amp; desist or court action.
:::
