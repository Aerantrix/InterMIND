<div class="gf-section gf-section-elevated">
  <div class="gf-container-narrow">
    <h2 class="gf-h2 gf-h2-center">Visa &amp; relocation questions, answered</h2>
    <p class="gf-sub gf-sub-center">Specific to UAE residency. General Golden Fish questions live on the <a href="/">homepage FAQ</a>.</p>
    <div class="gf-faq">

:::details Which visa do I qualify for?
Most clients qualify for one or two of: investor / company-owner visa, employer-sponsored visa for a hire of a UAE company, family sponsorship under a UAE-resident sponsor, or the 10-year Golden Visa. We don't run job-search or place candidates — the employer-sponsored route is only for UAE-registered companies sponsoring their own hires. The free eligibility assessment maps your situation to the best route based on cost, timeline and family scope.
:::

:::details How long does the visa process take?
Investor, employee and family-sponsorship visas typically run 2–3 weeks from submission to Emirates ID. Golden Visa applications run 4–6 weeks because of the authority review stage. Family sponsorship adds 1–2 weeks per dependent.
:::

:::details Do I need to fly to Dubai for the process?
Most of the paperwork is remote, but you need to be in the UAE for the medical fitness test, biometrics, and Emirates ID issuance. We coordinate the appointments so the in-person portion fits into 3–5 days.
:::

:::details Can I sponsor my parents and extended family?
Spouse and children under 25 are straightforward — parents are eligible under specific conditions (sponsor's salary, full medical insurance for parents, no other UAE-based child to sponsor them). We walk you through eligibility during the assessment call.
:::

:::details What is the Golden Visa and who qualifies?
The Golden Visa is a 10-year UAE residence visa with no sponsor required. The most common routes are: investor (AED 2M+ in property or business), exceptional talent (PhD, leading specialist, artist, scientist), and retirement (55+ with qualifying savings, property or pension). Family is included automatically.
:::

:::details What if I don't have a UAE company yet?
No problem — most clients either come in as employees (employer sponsors the visa), set up a UAE company in parallel (we coordinate this with our [company registration](/uae-business/offer/company-registration/) service), or qualify for the Golden Visa directly.
:::

:::details Can you help with school enrolment for my kids?
Yes. School enrolment is part of our "beyond visa" coordination — UAE, British, IB and American curricula, through our partner network. We help you shortlist schools and coordinate the application alongside your residency timeline.
:::

:::details What about medical insurance and Emirates ID?
Health insurance is mandatory for all UAE residents — we set this up with a partner broker once your visa is approved. Emirates ID is issued automatically as part of the visa process; we coordinate the biometrics appointment in Dubai.
:::
