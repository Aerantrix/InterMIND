    <h2 class="gf-h2 gf-h2-center">Company registration — common questions</h2>

    <div class="gf-faq">

:::details What's the absolute minimum cost to open a UAE company?
Around $1,500–3,000 for a JAFZA / RAK ICC offshore (no residency, holding-only). For an active business with a residence visa, the realistic floor is $3,500–4,500 in a low-cost free zone (e.g. SHAMS or IFZA Smart Office). Anything cheaper online usually omits visa, bank or yearly renewal costs.
:::

:::details Do I need to be in UAE physically to register?
No for licence and document preparation. Yes for one short trip (3–5 days) for biometrics (Emirates ID) and final visa stamping. We schedule everything in one block so you fly in once.
:::

:::details Free zone vs mainland — which is cheaper to maintain yearly?
Free zone is usually cheaper to maintain ($2,500–5,000 vs $3,500–8,000). Mainland gets more expensive because of physical office (Ejari) and DED renewal fees. The trade-off: mainland gives you direct UAE market access without a local distributor.
:::

:::details Can I open a UAE bank account 100% remotely?
Some digital banks (e.g. WIO) allow fully remote onboarding. Traditional banks (Emirates NBD, Mashreq, RAK Bank) typically require one in-person meeting. We arrange the schedule for you and prep all compliance docs in advance.
:::

:::details What if my activity isn't on the standard licence list?
Most free zones allow custom activity descriptions (with extra approval steps). Some industries (financial services, healthcare, education) require sector regulator approval — we handle the additional filing.
:::

:::details Can I add or remove shareholders later?
Yes. Adding: standard amendment, 1–2 weeks. Removing: depends on whether the exiting shareholder needs to be paid out for their stake (corporate restructuring) — we handle both.
:::

:::details Which documents need apostille / attestation?
Documents issued outside UAE that the licence authority requires (typically degrees, marriage certificates for spouse visas, parent company resolutions for branch setups). We issue a checklist with your specific list at the start.
:::

:::details How fast can it really be done — best case?
Free zone with 1 visa, no apostille, simple activity: 14–18 days licence + 7–10 days bank. Around 3.5 weeks end-to-end. Anything faster usually means corners cut on bank prep — and a higher risk of rejection.
:::

:::details What's the renewal cost yearly? Any hidden fees?
Renewal = licence + visa + office. Free zone: typically $2,500–5,000 yearly. Mainland: $3,500–8,000. We send the renewal quote 60 days in advance with the same line-item breakdown.
:::

:::details Can I upgrade from free zone to mainland later?
Yes — common path for businesses that start free zone for speed, then move to mainland once they need direct UAE market access. The free zone entity stays as IP holder; mainland entity does the local sales.
:::

:::details Corporate tax — do I really need to register?
Yes — registration is mandatory regardless of profit. The 9% rate only kicks in above AED 375K profit, but the registration deadline is independent of revenue. Penalties for late registration start at AED 10,000.
:::

:::details VAT — when is it required and when not?
Mandatory if your taxable revenue exceeds AED 375K in the past 12 months. Voluntary registration is allowed above AED 187.5K. Most early-stage companies wait until they cross the mandatory threshold.
:::
