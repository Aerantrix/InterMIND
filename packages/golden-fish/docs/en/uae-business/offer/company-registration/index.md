    <h2 class="gf-h2 gf-h2-center">Common questions</h2>
    <p class="gf-sub gf-sub-center">Specific to UAE company registration. General Golden Fish questions live on the <a href="/">homepage FAQ</a>.</p>
    <div class="gf-faq">

:::details What if scope changes mid-project?
Any change to scope is documented and quoted before work begins. You either approve in writing or we proceed with the original scope — no automatic add-ons, no scope creep.
:::

:::details Do you refund prepayment if I cancel?
Yes — refund conditions are spelled out in your written contract before you pay anything. Pre-approval costs (government filings already submitted) are non-refundable; the rest is refundable on a clearly defined schedule.
:::

:::details How long until I get my licence and bank account?
Free zone licence: typically 2–4 weeks. Bank account: 2–4 weeks after licence is issued. End-to-end: 4–6 weeks for most setups, longer for high-risk industries or multi-entity structures.
:::

:::details What if my bank application is rejected?
We open with 3+ banks in parallel to maximise approval odds. If all reject (rare — our overall approval rate is 94%), we re-prepare the file and resubmit at no extra cost as long as the rejection isn't due to undisclosed information from your side.
:::

:::details Will I pay corporate tax? VAT?
UAE corporate tax: 9% above AED 375k profit. VAT: 5% if revenue exceeds AED 375k. We register you for both as part of the Growth and Premium packages — and connect you with an accountant for ongoing filings.
:::

:::details Do I need to fly to Dubai for setup?
Most of the work — licence, document prep, initial bank application — is done remotely. You typically need to be in Dubai once for biometrics (Emirates ID) and final visa stamping. Total in-country time: 3–5 days.
:::

:::details Can my spouse and kids get visas too?
Yes. Once you hold a UAE residence visa, you can sponsor your spouse, children and (in some cases) parents. Family visa processing is included in our Premium package and available as an add-on for Essential and Growth.
:::

:::details What if I'm not a UAE resident yet?
That's the typical case. You don't need UAE residency to start the process — your residence visa is one of the outcomes of company registration, not a prerequisite.
:::
