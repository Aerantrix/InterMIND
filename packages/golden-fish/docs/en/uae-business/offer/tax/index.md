:::details Do I need Corporate Tax registration if my profit is below 375,000 AED?
Yes. Registration is required regardless of profit. The 9% Corporate Tax only applies on taxable income above AED 375,000 — but the registration obligation, annual return and supporting accounting are mandatory for almost all UAE entities. We handle registration as part of every Starter retainer.

:::details What if I'm behind on VAT or CT filings? Can you catch up?
Yes — catch-up engagements are common. We file overdue returns immediately, reconstruct records where needed, and negotiate penalty reductions with the FTA where the rules allow. Most catch-ups close in 2–6 weeks; complex multi-year cases take longer.

:::details How does QFZP (Qualifying Free Zone Person) status work?
QFZP allows qualifying free-zone entities to retain a 0% Corporate Tax rate on qualifying income. It requires substance, proper financial statements, and a clean separation of qualifying vs non-qualifying revenue. We assess eligibility, structure intra-group flows, and prepare the supporting documentation.
:::
